MaliBridge is built by one person, [ASEbrahim](https://github.com/ASEbrahim), but it would be nowhere without
the people who test it, send logs and report what breaks. Thank you, all of you.

Want to be on this list? Test a game, [send logs](README.md#getting-help), or post your results in
[Device reports](https://github.com/ASEbrahim/MaliBridge/discussions/2) or on [Discord](https://discord.gg/9xC67qTjDq).
